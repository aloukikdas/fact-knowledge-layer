How It Works

Automated Due Diligence in 3 Core Steps

1. Footnote-Aware Ingestion

PyMuPDF extracts text and scans from the bottom-up to tie detached footnotes back to financial tables, preventing out-of-context hallucinations.

2. Strict Fact Contracts

Extracted numbers are validated against strict Pydantic schemas (canonical metric, reporting period, accounting standard, and exact page citations).

3. Contextual Arbitration

The engine clusters metrics and differentiates genuine contradictions from apparent ones reconciled by time (stub vs. full year) or reporting scope.
